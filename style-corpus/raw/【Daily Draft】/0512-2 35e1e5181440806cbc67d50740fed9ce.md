ONE OF THE FIRST MEMBERS OF MODERN MATURITY CENTER SHES BEEN COMING HERE SINCE 1973.

Im over a hundred. A hundred and eight

YES SHES 108 YEARS OLD SHE STILL JOINS GROUP WORKOUTS THREE TIMES A WEEK. TO PROVE IT SHE PROVIDED US HER DRIVERS LISCENCE. AND YES, SHE STILL DRIVES.

They renewed my drivers license until 2033

SHE SAYS THE KEY TO A HEALTHY LIFE IS MOVING.

When I get up in the morning. I get down on the floor and I have an exercise routine that Ive been doing for the last 20 years.

BORN IN 1918 IN HOUSTON, DELAWARE MS. BROWNE GREW UP DURING SEGREGATION, HELPING HER FAMILY ON A FARM WITHOUT RUNNING WATER OR ELECTRICITY.

SHE LATER ATTENDED DELAWARE STATE COLLEGE FOR COLORED STUDENTS AND AFTER GRADUATING IN 1945 SPENT 30 YEARS TEACHING CHILDREN ACROSS DELAWARE, INCLUDING IN A ONE-ROOM SCHOOLHOUSE.

When I retired and I walked around that classroom for 30 years I am not going to sit down

SHES BEEN MARRIED TWICE AND IS KNOWN FOR HER SENSE OF HUMOR.

I guess Im not great material for men

That was enough Im not taking care another man

FROM HER FAMOUS SEVEN-UP POUND CAKE TO HER FRONT-ROW SEAT AT A 108TH BIRTHDAY PARTY ATTENDED BY MORE THAN 130 PEOPLE INCLUDING GOVERNOR MATT MEYER MS. BROWNE CONTINUES TO MAKE AN ENTRANCE EVERYWHERE SHE GOES.

I grow old gracefully

AND FOR SOMEONE WHOS LIVED MORE THAN A CENTURY THE PERFECT BIRTHDAY GIFT?

HER VERY OWN RESERVED PARKING SPOT FOR DRIVERS OVER 100.
