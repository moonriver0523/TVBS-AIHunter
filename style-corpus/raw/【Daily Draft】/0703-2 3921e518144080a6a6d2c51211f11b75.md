（受訪者）我們相信美國仍是我們的親密朋友，且我們共享價值——在7月4日美國國慶日前夕特別重要——包括生存權。我們希望美國人民站在我們這一邊，也希望美國總統基於至少人道理由，更積極提供必要支援。當然，我們感謝自全面入侵以來已提供的一切，但我們需要更多支持，也不願失去美國這位最親密的夥伴與朋友。

（Aleksandr Mareshko）We believe that the United States remains our close friend, and we share common values—important to emphasize on the eve of the 4th of July—including the right to life. We hope the American people remain on our side and that the American president will be more active, at least for humanitarian reasons, in providing the necessary support. Of course, we are grateful for everything that has already been done during the full-scale invasion, but we need more support, and we would not like to lose the United States as our closest partner and friend.

06:07:09-06:07:39

（主播）你仍真心抱有希望嗎？你們已等待多時，而川普政府對烏克蘭的一貫政策是削減援助——軍事、財政與其他方面。你仍相信美國總統認同烏克蘭的價值並關心烏克蘭防衛嗎？

（Host）Do you still have genuine hope for that? You’ve been waiting, and one consistent part of the Trump administration’s policy toward Ukraine has been reducing aid—military assistance, financial assistance, and more. Do you still believe the U.S. president shares Ukraine’s values and cares about Ukraine’s defense?

06:07:39-06:08:32

（受訪者）首先，美國持續向我們提供必要的情報。美國也出售我們所需武器，而歐洲夥伴願意在財務上支持我們購買這些武器。此外，美國國會（U.S. Congress）有一項法案規劃對烏克蘭的更多支援。我們收到非常明確的訊號，至少美國社會與美國國會堅定站在烏克蘭一邊。但我們當然需要持續與川普總統（Donald Trump）溝通，向他說明並說服他，持續支持烏克蘭符合美國的最大利益，因為俄羅斯不僅在對烏克蘭開戰，也在對人類、以及歐洲與美國的安全開戰。

（Aleksandr Mareshko）First of all, the United States continues to provide us with necessary intelligence information. The United States sells the weaponry we need, and our European partners are ready to support us financially to buy this weaponry. Also, there is a bill in the U.S. Congress that envisages more support for Ukraine. We have very clear signals that at least American society and the American Congress are firmly on the side of Ukraine. But of course we need to continue to work with President Trump, to explain and persuade him that it is in the best interest of the United States to continue supporting Ukraine against Russia, which is waging war not only against Ukraine but against humanity and the security of Europe and the United States.
