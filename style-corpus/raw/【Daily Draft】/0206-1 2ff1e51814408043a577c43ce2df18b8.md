CNN記者 Mike Valerio：「跩哥．馬份跟中國的好運有什麼關係」
What does Draco Malfoy have to do with good luck in China?

電影哈利波特片段：「跩哥．馬份」
NATS: Draco Malfoy.

CNN記者 Mike Valerio：「在農曆新年開始前，這些裝飾品已經在中國的社群媒體上到處可見。農曆新年是在2月17日登場，當然也象徵著中國生肖中的馬年到來。這股風潮甚至引起了湯姆費爾頓（Tom Felton）的注意，他是英國演員，曾在哈利波特電影系列中飾演這個角色，還在他的 Instagram 限時動態分享了這件事。
這股突如其來的人氣，全都要歸功於「馬份」在中文裡的翻譯，也就是「馬爾福」，其中「馬」代表馬，「福」則象徵好運與福氣。中國各地的人們有個傳統，會在自家大門上貼上「福」字，希望在新的一年獲得祝福與好運。
所以我就在想，為什麼在蛇年的時候，馬份沒有紅遍各地？畢竟他可是史萊哲林的人。不過遲來總比不到好。新年快樂！Happy New Year!」

So these decorations have popped up all over Chinese social media ahead of the start of the Lunar New Year. That starts on February 17th, and of course it ushers in the year of the horse in the Chinese zodiac. It even got the attention of Tom Felton, the English actor who played the character in the Harry Potter movie series, sharing this on his Instagram story. All this sudden popularity is thanks to the Chinese translation of Malfoy, which is 'ma er fu' with 'ma' meaning horse and 'fu' meaning good fortune. It's a tradition for people across China to put the character 'fu' on their front doors in hopes of blessings and good fortune in the year ahead. So I wonder why Malfoy wasn't everywhere during the year of the snake, since he is a Slytherin. Well, better late than never. Xīnnián kuàilè. Happy New Year!
