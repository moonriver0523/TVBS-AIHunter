在狹小、燈光昏暗的地面控制站裡，隸屬於美國北達科他州國民衛隊、呼號「快樂頑童」的飛行員，正操控MQ-9「死神」無人機。這款無人飛行載具，可以透過遠端操控，或由電腦自主飛行。

記者vs.美軍無人機操縱員：「那就是機上的攝影機。對，攝影機裝在機鼻前方，有點像在飛機的下巴位置，我們可以用它觀察四周所有方向。」

美國空軍將「死神」稱為一種「情報蒐集資產」。它能夠掛載數百磅武器，燃料充足時，最長可在空中滯留約24小時。

機組人員全年無休、無論天候狀況，都在美國中西部上空執行各式訓練任務。

記者採訪當天，這組人員正與數百英里外的海軍部隊進行聯合演訓。

美軍無人機操縱員：「我們剛剛穿越雲層，上升過程沒有遇到結冰情況，這真的很棒。現在就是在這一帶找個合適的位置盤旋。」

MQ-9的另一項特色，是具備自動起降能力，也就是ATLC系統。透過電腦與衛星技術，「死神」可以在飛行員監控下，自行完成起飛與降落。

第119作戰群指揮官 Lee Teigen中將：「飛機本身內建『可飛/不可飛』的安全機制，確保所有參數都符合標準。如果條件不符，系統就會中止起飛或降落；一旦達標，就會啟動自動化程序，按照預設飛行任務自行起飛。」

在控制中心內，機組人員緊盯多個監視器，上面顯示關鍵資訊，包括飛機位置、速度、高度、燃油狀況、引擎狀態以及航圖資料。這次任務期間，天氣看來並未造成太大影響。

美軍無人機操縱員：「等一下我們會進行一些訓練。今天會跟在美國另一端的海軍人員合作。現在只需要找到地面上視野清楚的位置就可以了。」

INSIDE A CRAMPED, DIMLY-LIT GROUND CONTROL STATION IN FARGO, PILOTS WITH THE NORTH DAKOTA AIR NATIONAL GUARD’S HAPPY HOOLIGANS FLY THE MQ-9 REAPER, AN UNMANNED AERIAL VEHICLE THAT’S FLOWN REMOTELY OR AUTONOMOUSLY BY COMPUTER.

So that's the camera on board. Yeah, the camera's on the front. It's kind of on the chin of the airplane, and then we can look all around.

THE AIR FORCE CALLS THE REAPER AN “INTELLIGENCE-COLLECTION ASSET.”

IT’S CAPABLE OF CARRYING HUNDREDS OF POUNDS OF WEAPONS AND HAS ENOUGH FUEL TO REMAIN IN THE AIR FOR AROUND 24 HOURS.

CREWMEMERS KEEP BUSY THROUGHOUT THE YEAR, IN ALL KINDS OF WEATHER, FLYING TRAINING MISSIONS ACROSS NORTH DAKOTA, MINNESOTA, WISCONSIN AND THE UPPER MIDWEST.

DURING OUR VISIT, THE CREW WAS INVOLVED IN A JOINT EXERCISE WITH THE NAVY, HUNDREDS OF MILES AWAY.

THEIR NAMES ARE NOT BEING DISCLOSED FOR SECURITY REASONS.

(no name provided): "We just went up through the clouds out there, didn't pick up any ice on the way up, which is wonderful, and now we're just kind of finding a place to run around here."

ANOTHER FEATURE OF THE MQ-9 IS AUTOMATIC LANDING AND TAKEOFF CAPABILITY, ALSO KNOWN AS ATLC.

COMPUTERS AND SATELLITES ENABLE THE REAPER TO TAKE OFF AND LAND–ALL ON ITS OWN–WHILE UNDER THE WATCHFUL EYES OF A PILOT.

LT. COL. LEE TEIGEN IS THE 119TH OPERATIONS GROUP COMMANDER.

Lt. Col. Lee Teigen: "There's internal go no go safe features that the aircraft has itself to make sure all the parameters are met. If the parameters are not met, it will abort the takeoff or landing. If they are met, it will start the automation process and the aircraft will take off on that pre-programmed flight mission."

INSIDE THE CONTROL CENTER, THE CREW KEEPS BUSY WATCHING AN ARRAY OF MONITORS THAT DISPLAY CRITICAL INFORMATION, INCLUDING THE AIRCRAFT’S POSITION, SPEED, ALTITUDE, FUEL, ENGINE STATUS AND AIR CHARTS.

THE WEATHER DURING THIS PARTICULAR MISSION DID NOT SEEM TO PRESENT ANY PROBLEMS.
