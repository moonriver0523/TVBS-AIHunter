#AICG 3-2

SB #04
CNBC報導：「不同於傳統網路衛星，體積如校車、運行於距地約3.6萬公里的軌道，Starlink 衛星體積更小，且軌道更低，大約在距地550公里。但因為距離地面更近，每顆衛星同一時間能覆蓋的地表範圍較小，這也是為什麼SpaceX需要部署大量衛星。另一方面，SpaceX 表示，這種較低軌道的優勢在於可以降低訊號延遲，讓網路反應速度更快。」
0400-0426
Unlike traditional internet satellites which are as big as a school bus and orbit at around 36,000 km above Earth's surface, Starlink satellites are much smaller and closer at about 550 km above Earth's surface. But the satellites closer placement means they can see less of the Earth at any given point in time, which is why SpaceX needs so many. On the flip side, SpaceX claims that this closer orbit allows the system to have a lower signal delay, known as latency.
