來看美國加州這件超高難度空中救援，有一名修剪樹木的工人身體不適，受困7層樓高的棕櫚樹上，長達1.5小時，當局出動直升機，由吊掛方式，成功把他轉移到地面，詳細情形，交給國際組記者許岱軒。

直升機吊掛救難人員，從空中緩緩接近他們要拯救的目標，這次救援任務罕見超高難度，因為目標受困在一棵23公尺，超過七層樓高的棕櫚樹上。

救難人員成功垂吊抓住受困者，立刻用繩索固定他，切除他身上本來的安全繩。

這是在美國加州聖荷西，這位修剪棕櫚樹的工人，工作到一半不明原因身體不適，需要緊急醫療，受困樹上一個半小時。當地沒有23公尺高的雲梯車，只好用直升機救援。

SB #01
KGO記者 Karina Nova：「Sky7電視台直升機現場全程直播，捕捉令人屏息的瞬間，同時加州消防隊直升機也趕來協助救援。」
0020-0028
SKY7 WAS LIVE OVER THE SCENE SHOWING EVERY HEART-STOPPING MOMENT, AS A CAL FIRE HELICOPTER ARRIVED TO ASSIST IN THE RESCUE.

整個過程要在幾分鐘之內迅速完成，多待一秒更危險，因為直升機旋翼會產生下沉氣流，棕櫚樹搖晃，可能撐不住斷裂。

SB #01
KGO記者 Karina Nova：「過程中一度出現驚險下沉的瞬間……加上樹體搖晃，形成特殊挑戰。」
0054-0057
AT ONE POINT... A HEART STOPPING DIP... AND THE SWAYING TREE CREATING A UNIQUE CHALLENGE.

SB #01
聖塔克拉拉消防分隊長 Marcus Hernandez：「我們擔心直升機旋翼下洗氣流會讓樹進一步晃動，但我們的團隊已經準備好應對各種動態救援情境，而這正是一個典型案例。」
0057-0108
One of our concerns was that rotor watch coming down causing the tree to move additionally, but our crews are prepared for rescues in dynamic situations and that was exactly one of those dynamic situations.”

幸好這場高難度救援任務成功，6分鐘內，救難人員將順利受困的剪樹工人垂吊轉移到地面上，立刻將他送醫治療，眾人這才鬆一口氣。當地消防隊員直呼，這是25年生涯中，最困難的救援行動。

Nats

TENSE MOMENTS IN SAN JOSE FRIDAY MORNING ... AS A TREE TRIMMER FOUND HIMSELF NEEDING TO BE RESCUED -- HANGING 75 FEET IN THE AIR.

James Dobson/ Assistant Fire Chief, San Jose Fire Department: "Because of the height, greater than 75 feet, and distance away from the street, it made the rescue a very challenging rescue."

SAN JOSE FIRE SAYS TIME WAS OF THE ESSENCE AFTER THE WORKER SUFFERED AN UNKNOWN MEDICAL EMERGENCY WHILE PRUNING A PALM TREE.

THAT LEFT HIM STRANDED THERE FOR OVER AN HOUR.

A CAL FIRE RESCUER EVENTUALLY ABLE TO LOWER HIMSELF FROM A HELICOPTER ... SWINGING THROUGH THE AIR UNTIL HE COULD HOOK ONTO THE TREE -- WORKING OVER SEVERAL MINUTES TO SAFELY SECURE THE MAN AND CUT AWAY HIS OTHER HARNESS.

AT ONE POINT... A HEART STOPPING DIP... AND THE SWAYING TREE CREATING A UNIQUE CHALLENGE.

Marcus Hernandez/ Unit Chief, Cal Fire Santa Clara Unit: "One of our concerns was that rotor watch coming down causing the tree to move additionally, but our crews are prepared for rescues in dynamic situations and that was exactly one of those dynamic situations."

BUT THAT DYNAMIC SITUATION ENDED WITH A MOMENT OF RELIEF.

AFTER SIX MINUTES, THE TWO ARE LOWERED TO THE GROUND AND MET BY PARAMEDICS.

CAL FIRE DEEMING THIS RESCUE A SUCCESS DUE IN PART TO THE MAN'S SAFETY HARNESS.

Marcus Hernandez/ Unit Chief, Cal Fire Santa Clara Unit: "Very lucky, any time you have a decrease in consciousness, you're going to want have some safety equipment anchoring you, just for this type of situation. So, very lucky he had a safety device on him."

垂降救援
WE-013FR (SOT)
加州聖荷西週五上演驚險高空救援，一名樹木修剪工人在約75英尺高處作業時突發不明醫療狀況，受困樹上逾一小時。由於高度高且距離街道遠，增加救援難度。消防單位與CAL FIRE 出動直升機支援，救援人員從空中垂降接近目標，在搖晃的樹上固定傷者並切除安全繩索。過程中旋翼氣流造成樹體擺動，一度出現驚險下沉畫面。最終約6分鐘內成功將兩人降落地面，由醫護人員接手。消防單位表示，傷者因配戴安全吊帶而得以保命，整起救援行動順利完成。
