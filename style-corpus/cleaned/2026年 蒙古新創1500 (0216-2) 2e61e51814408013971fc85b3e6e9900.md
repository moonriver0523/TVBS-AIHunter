開車尋找牲畜一週，可能就要花上數百美元，對牧民家庭來說是一筆沉重負擔，在極端天氣下甚至可能危及生命。
A week-long search for livestock by car can cost hundreds of dollars, a significant cost for her families and can be dangerous in extreme weather.

在蒙古，全國有26%的土地沒有行動通訊網路。由於蒙古人是遊牧民族，約有一半人口在四季分明、氣候極端的環境中，無法取得網路。如果持續沿用傳統方式、卻不善用科技來對抗氣候變遷，我們就會被時代淘汰，必須想出保護人民與牲畜、免於火災與天災的解方。
For Mongolia, 26% of the territory has no mobile network. As Mongols are nomads, half of them have no network access in an extreme extreme climate with four seasons. If we continue in such traditional ways without using technology to combat climate change, we will be left behind. We have to come up with a solution to protect people and livestock from fire and natural disasters.

 which will allow continuous realtime reliable connectivity around the world no matter where you are.

立方衛星並非新技術，其他公司如 Spotter Mongolia 也已在當地提供牲畜追蹤服務，但 Onodo Space 認為自己能善用一些獨特條件。
Now cubats have been around for a while and other companies like Spotter Mongolia also offer livestock tracking services here. But Onondo Space says it can take advantage of some unique circumstances.

我們的成本大約比全球平均低3.5倍、速度也更快，原因包括對商業太空公司非常友善的法規環境、優秀的工程團隊，以及蒙古獨特的地理位置，使我們在採購全球零組件時不受貿易限制。
We do it about 3.5 times cheaper and faster than the global average based on a number of reasons such as our uh regulatory environment which is very friendly for commercial space companies. 他們的計畫，是建立一套自有網路，將類似 GPS 的數據傳送到智慧型手機等裝置，即使在沒有網路覆蓋的地區也能使用。
The plan is to create their own network for sending data like a GPS signal to a device like a smartphone, even in areas with no internet connectivity.

今天，就在首都郊外，他們首次進行牲畜的即時實地測試，為馬匹戴上特製項圈，在草原上進行追蹤；第二次嘗試時，系統成功接收到訊號。
Today, just outside of the capital, they're doing the first live testing of their system on livestock by attaching specially designed collars to horses to track them out on the planes and on the second attempt the system picks up the signal.

科技的引入不會改變這種生活方式，但能節省時間，尤其在緊急情況下，能幫助牧民保命，也為他們帶來經濟效益。
The introduction of technology won't change this lifestyle, but it will help save time, especially in cases of emergency. It will help herders by saving their lives and will create economic benefits for them as well.

目前仍在起步階段，他們也不是第一個將衛星帶進草原的企業。伊隆・馬斯克的 Starlink 近期已在蒙古上線，提供更高容量的數據連線。不過，作為蒙古第一家本土衛星公司，Onodo Space 擁有獨特的蒙古視角。
It's still early days and they're not the first to bring satellites to the step. Elon Musk's Starlink has recently become available here and offers a much higher volume of data connectivity. But as the country's first homegrown satellite company, Onondo Space has a uniquely Mongolian perspective.

In my opinion, Mongols cannot be understood separately from horses. Historically, the horse was the best technology in the 13th century.
