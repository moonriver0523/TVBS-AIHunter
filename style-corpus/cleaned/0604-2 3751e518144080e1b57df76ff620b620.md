- **-LEAD IN**-

TEENS FACE MULTIPLE HEADWINDS AS THEY HUNT FOR SUMMER JOBS.

ONE FORECAST POINTS TO THE WEAKEST SUMMER FOR TEEN HIRING IN ALMOST 80 YEARS --

BUT NEW DATA POINTS TO POSSIBLE OPENINGS LATER IN THE SUMMER MONTHS.

KARIN CAIFA (PRONO: KAY-FUH) IS IN WASHINGTON WITH A LOOK AT THE OBSTACLES FOR YOUNG WORKERS -- AND POSSIBLE OPPORTUNITIES.

- **-REPORTER PKG-AS FOLLOWS**-

THE SUMMER JOB MARKET FOR TEENS AND YOUNG WORKERS --

ISN'T SO HOT.

ACCORDING TO OUTPLACEMENT AND CONSULTING FIRM CHALLENGER, GRAY AND CHRISTMAS --

20-26 COULD BE THE WORST SUMMER FOR TEEN HIRING SINCE THE GOVERNMENT STARTED KEEPING TRACK IN 19-48.

ANDY CHALLENGER SAYS SECTORS THAT TYPICALLY HIRE TEENS -- ARE TAKING A HIT.

Andy Challenger, Challenger, Gray & Christmas Chief Revenue Officer: "This year, inflation, high oil prices are hitting the handful of industries like leisure and entertainment - that extra pocket money spending during the summer - where teenagers tend to find most of the summer jobs."

THERE'S ALSO COMPETITION FROM RECENT COLLEGE GRADS WHO HAVE STRUGGLED TO LAND PERMANENT GIGS --

AND FROM WORKERS OF RETIREMENT AGE, WHO ARE INCREASINGLY SEEKING PART-TIME AND SEASONAL ROLES.

AND -- AUTOMATION.

Andy Challenger, Challenger, Gray & Christmas Chief Revenue Officer: "A lot of companies like fast food restaurants and convenience stores have made investments over the last few years in self-service kiosks, checkouts, those typical jobs that would go to teenagers."

NICOLE BACHAUD, LABOR ECONOMIST AT ZIPRECRUITER, SAYS EMPLOYERS HAVE PLAYED IT SAFE WITH HIRING -- IN AN UNCERTAIN ECONOMY.

Nicole Bachaud, Ziprecruiter Labor Economist: "Stability's been the name of the game for the last year and a half, uh, maintaining their workforce, not really looking to expand, which has really been limiting entry-level opportunities."

BUT BACHAUD ALSO NOTES JOB GROWTH HAS VERY RECENTLY BROADENED -- TO INCLUDE MORE SECTORS THAT TYPICALLY HIRE YOUNG WORKERS.

Nicole Bachaud, Ziprecruiter Labor Economist: "That includes things like transportation and retail. Despite high gas prices, we're still seeing some signals that employers are increasing demand for employment in those areas.

- - MEANING MORE OPPORTUNITIES COULD OPEN, AS SUMMER GOES ON.

IN WASHINGTON,I'M KARIN CAIFA

青少年暑期打工，正面臨多重逆風。

一項預測指出，今年暑假可能是近80年來，青少年就業最疲弱的一季。

不過最新數據也顯示，到了夏季後段，可能仍會出現一些工作機會。

記者卡琳・凱法（Karin Caifa）在華府，帶我們看看年輕勞工面臨的阻礙，以及可能出現的機會。

美國正在放暑假，青少年與年輕勞工的暑期就業市場，並不算熱絡。根據就業轉職與顧問公司指出，2026年可能會是有統計以來，青少年暑期雇用最差的一年。

SB  0020-0034
就業轉職顧問公司營收長 Andy Challenger
「今年通膨、高油價，正在打擊少數幾個產業，例如休閒娛樂，也就是夏季大家用零用錢消費的領域，而青少年通常最常在這些產業找到暑期工作。」

此外，他們也面臨剛畢業大學生的競爭，這些大學生難以找到穩定全職工作。

還有來自退休年齡勞工的競爭，這些人越來越常尋找兼職或季節性工作。雪上加霜的，是自動化衝擊。

SB  0047-0058
就業轉職顧問公司營收長 Andy Challenger
「很多公司，像是速食店和便利商店，過去幾年投資了自助點餐機、自助結帳設備，這些原本通常會給青少年的工作。」

勞動經濟學家分析，在經濟不確定之下，雇主在招募上一直採取保守態度。

SB  0105-0114
勞動經濟學家 Nicole Bachaud
「過去一年半以來，穩定一直是最重要的原則，企業維持現有人力，並沒有真的想擴編，這也大幅限制了入門職缺的機會。」

最近就業成長已經擴大到更多產業，其中包括通常會雇用年輕勞工的領域。

SB 0121-0130
勞動經濟學家 Nicole Bachaud
「這包括像是運輸業和零售業。儘管油價高，我們仍看到一些跡象，顯示這些領域的雇主正在增加用人需求。」

不過根據預估，美國夏天尾聲，可能會有更多就業機會。
