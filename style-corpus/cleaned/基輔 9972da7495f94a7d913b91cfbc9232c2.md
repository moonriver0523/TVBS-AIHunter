Multiple explosions shook Kyiv on Monday morning following weeks of relative calm in the Ukrainian capital. Other cities in Ukraine have also been hit by a large-scale Russian bombardment. Here are the latest developments:

- **Strikes hit Kyiv:** The mayor of Kyiv has urged residents to remain in shelters today, after a [children's playground](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_f396a6e685c63369315dfcd3936ef0c2) was among the sites hit by four apparent [Russian strikes](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_247d30cedff01266f69bd63e71a45910) that left [multiple people dead](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_d28f8b25a2e711a7ffaadb4ff69bb884).
- **Blasts rock Ukrainian cities:** Regional authorities [reported](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_fa35114024e6559cb1c5d541384adbe2) missile and rocket attacks in Kharkiv, Lviv, Mykolaiv and Dnipropetrovsk on Monday morning, which appear to have been partly directed at power plants, bridges and [other civilian infrastructure](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_255eff01e1c12229172d4259095171e2).
- **Global leaders condemn Moscow:** European leaders [criticized](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_3ccecdb040fc4d73a9997effc376d1ae) "Russia's relentless attacks" in the southeastern city of Zaporizhzhia, while Ukrainian Foreign Minister Dmytro Kuleba [said](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_fa35114024e6559cb1c5d541384adbe2) Russian President Vladimir Putin "is a terrorist who talks with missiles," in the wake of the blasts in Kyiv.
- **Putin to chair Russia Security council meeting:** The Russian president will hold [an operational meeting](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_95cb20647e445889fb9d06bfc90b1474) of his Security Council on Monday, just two days after [a massive explosion](https://www.cnn.com/2022/10/08/europe/crimea-bridge-putin-ukraine-analysis-intl/index.html) on a key strategic bridge linking Crimea and Russia.
- **Beijing calls for de-escalation in Ukraine:** China has expressed hope that the situation in Ukraine will be [“de-escalated soon.”](https://www.cnn.com/europe/live-news/russia-ukraine-war-news-10-10-22/h_77a4cd5abc05019ae6b09e09ac9883ac)

Strikes across Ukraine this morning show that Russia is "trying to destroy us and wipe us off the face of the Earth," Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky says.

In a Telegram post, Zelensky highlights blasts in Kyiv, Zaporizhzhia and Dnipro, saying "the air alarm does not subside throughout Ukraine."

"Unfortunately, there are dead and wounded," he adds, before urging his compatriots to stay in shelters.

Russia has not yet commented on this morning's strikes.
