美政府監管緊縮 AI新模型上市前30日審查

（圖／達志影像美聯社）

SB CNN 6/3

CNN主播 Richard Quest
川普希望美國聯邦政府對先進AI模型有一定監督。這是一道新的行政命令，要求業界提供30天審查期，讓政府檢視最新系統。期間，政府將判斷是否先由特定可信任夥伴試用。起因是Mythos。Anthropic已警告其Mythos模型可被用來利用軟體漏洞——這正是其用途。公司現再開放給150家夥伴存取Mythos，讓他們優先找出弱點。該公司昨遞件規劃首次公開募股IPO。
040913-040951
And Trump wants the US federal government to have some oversight of advanced AI models. It's a new executive order, and it asks the industry for a 30-day window to review their latest systems. During that time, the government will identify whether the AI should first be used by select trusted partners. This is all because of Mythos. Anthropic has already warned that its Mythos model could be used to exploit software bugs. That's its purpose. It's now giving 150 more partners access to Mythos so they can identify those vulnerabilities first. The company filed plans yesterday for an initial public offering.

CNN記者 Clare Duffy
正是Anthropic的Mythos促使政府從原本較少干預，轉向加強對AI公司的監督。Anthropic警告，如你所說，Mythos等同於數百名駭客24小時運作，能找出大量資安弱點。即使不是Anthropic的Mythos，其他AI公司也在追趕推出類似模型。這也是Anthropic自四月啟動的限量釋出計畫Project Glasswing的動機，當時只開放給50家夥伴，包括金融與資安公司。合作夥伴共發現1萬個可因Mythos而修補的資安漏洞。現在擴大到額外150家夥伴——涵蓋電力、自來水、醫療、通訊等關鍵產業的公司與機構。他們希望在技術廣泛開放前，先協助關鍵產業找出弱點。
041014-041119
It really is this Mythos model from Anthropic that has encouraged the government to shift from what was a more hands-off approach to oversight of these AI companies. Anthropic has warned that, as you said, Mythos could create huge opportunities for hackers — essentially like having hundreds of hackers working 24/7 who could identify security vulnerabilities. And if it's not Anthropic's Mythos, there are other AI companies close behind with similar models. That was the motivation behind Project Glasswing, the limited-release program Anthropic has been working on since April, when it rolled this out to just 50 partners, including finance and cybersecurity companies. Its partners identified 10,000 security vulnerabilities that it was able to fix because of Mythos. Now they're rolling it out to 150 additional partners — companies and organizations in critical industries like power, water, health care, communications. They want to help these critical industries identify vulnerabilities before this technology becomes widely available. 

And I think the same is true of this Trump executive order: they want the government to have a sense of what is coming and potentially to give important companies a chance to see what's coming before this technology is widely available — potentially to bad actors.

**04:11:33-04:12:06（主播 Richard）**

好，不過難題很多：讓夥伴提前接觸的常識性做法，與第一修正案權利、以及不想讓政府監看之間的拉扯。我們正踏入新領域。無論怎麼稱呼這個政府，基於其肩負我們多數人沒有的責任，或許該給予善意推定。

**(Richard)** Okay, but I guess the difficulty here — I mean, there are so many, in a sense. The common sense of giving access to partners in advance versus First Amendment rights, versus you don't want the government snooping. But we are in new territory here, and I guess the administration, whatever you want to call it, must be given the benefit of the doubt, in that they have a responsibility the rest of us do not.

**04:12:06-04:12:52（記者 Claire Duffy）**

這道行政命令有趣之處在於它其實是自願性監督計畫，要求公司自願給政府30天，在更廣泛發布前審視模型；而且是與多家大型AI實驗室合作制定。不過你會記得，Richard，川普在兩週前、原訂簽署前夕曾暫緩此命令。較早版本給政府90天審查，但業界人士認為時間過長，因為AI模型演進極快，等完90天，可能得審另一套新模型。因此最終版本是與產業協調後的結果。

**(Claire Duffy)** Well, what's interesting about this executive order is it's actually a voluntary oversight program. It's asking companies to voluntarily give the government 30 days to look over their models before they are released more widely, and it was developed in partnership with many of the big AI labs. But you'll remember, Richard, that Trump hit pause on this executive order two weeks ago, just before he was set to sign it. An earlier version would have given the government 90 days to review these models, and members of the industry said that is just too much time because AI models are accelerating and improving so quickly that by the time you finish a 90-day review, you might have something entirely new to look at. So this was created in partnership with the industry.
