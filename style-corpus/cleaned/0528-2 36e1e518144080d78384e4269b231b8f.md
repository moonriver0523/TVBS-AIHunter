美國德州聖體城這個學區史上首次，由高中生親手打造的飛機，本週二上午成功升空。
數十名學生和教職員聚集在道路旁，目送同學們的作品飛上天空。
飛機降落在聖體城國際機場後，參與製作、也親自搭乘試飛的學生，難掩興奮。

SB  
00:24 - 00:34
德州航空課學生 Barrett Matthews
「感覺真的很不真實。花三年做一架飛機，最後真的看到它飛起來，太瘋狂了。過程中遇到很多問題，有時候會覺得，這東西怎麼可能飛？畢竟是學生做的。但當你真的坐在上面飛的時候，只會想：天啊，這太不可思議了」
"It was surreal. Like I said earlier, It's crazy to like work on something for 3 years and then actually see it work, because you know, you run into so many problems and you're like, there's no way this thing's gonna fly. Like, it's built by kids, and then you're flying over and you're like, oh my God, this is crazy!”

這項計畫一開始只有一箱零件，歷經將近三年完成，期間由好幾屆學生接力打造。
對航空教師來說，學生的努力終於升空，就是最大的肯定。

SB
00:58 - 01:05
德州航空課教師 Caleb Harris
「對我和學生來說，真正的里程碑一直都是飛起來。所以能看到這一刻，真的很棒。」
"The whole time though, for me and for the students, the, the real milestone is flying, and so that's, uh, that's really good to see.

而對參與左機翼製作、也親自搭機體驗的高三學來說，這不只是一次成功試飛。更是讓計畫延續下去的開始。

SB
01:15 - 01:24
德州航空課學生 Jolina Matos
「這是第一架飛機。我希望因為它的成功，這個計畫能繼續下去，讓更多人有機會擁有和我一樣的經驗。」
"This is the first plane, and I hope that. Because of this and the success that has happened with this plane, we'll be able to keep the program alive and going, and more people will have the same opportunity that I've been given.”

學區總監表示，這次試飛展現的，正是技職教育的價值。更是未來實作教育的藍圖。

SB
01:36 - 01:46
德州學區總監 Chris Steinbrook：
「這就是職業技術教育應該有的樣子。學生在兩年時間裡，和世界一流的航空專業人員合作，一起打造出這架飛機。」
"Well, this is exactly what career technical education should look like, so our students over the, in the course of 2 years, were able to work with the most skilled aviation professionals in the world to build this airplane.”

鄰近的聖體城陸軍維修基地，也提供專業技術和指導，協助學生把夢想變成現實。這項合作也展現當地深厚的航空傳統。

FOR THE FIRST TIME IN FLOUR BLUFF ISD HISTORY, A PLANE BUILT BY HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS TOOK TO THE SKIES TUESDAY MORNING.

DOZENS OF STUDENTS AND STAFF LINED WALDRON ROAD, WATCHING AS THEIR FELLOW HORNETS' CREATION SOARED OVERHEAD.

AFTER THE PLANE TOUCHED BACK DOWN AT CORPUS CHRISTI INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT, I CAUGHT UP WITH STUDENT BARRETT MATTHEWS -- WHO HELPED BUILD THE AIRCRAFT AND GOT TO EXPERIENCE HIS CREATION FROM THE SKY.

Barrett Matthews, Aviation Student:

"It was surreal. Like I said earlier, It's crazy to like work on something for 3 years and then actually see it work, because you know, you run into so many problems and you're like, there's no way this thing's gonna fly. Like, it's built by kids, and then you're flying over and you're like, oh my God, this is crazy!"

THE PROJECT I FIRST REPORTED ON IN FEBRUARY STARTED WITH JUST A SINGLE CRATE OF PARTS AND TOOK NEARLY THREE YEARS TO COMPLETE -- WITH MULTIPLE CLASSES OF STUDENTS ROTATING THROUGH THE BUILD.

FOR AVIATION INSTRUCTOR CALEB HARRIS, SEEING HIS STUDENTS' HARD WORK FINALLY TAKE FLIGHT WAS THE ULTIMATE VALIDATION.

Caleb Harris, Instructor:

"The whole time though, for me and for the students, the, the real milestone is flying, and so that's, uh, that's really good to see.

BUT FOR SENIOR JOLINA MATOS, WHO WORKED ON THE LEFT WING AND EXPERIENCED THE FLIGHT FIRSTHAND --

THIS HISTORIC MOMENT IS JUST THE FOUNDATION FOR SOMETHING BIGGER.

Jolina Matos, Aviation Student:

"This is the first plane, and I hope that. Because of this and the success that has happened with this plane, we'll be able to keep the program alive and going, and more people will have the same opportunity that I've been given."

FOR SUPERINTENDENT CHRIS STEINBROOK, TUESDAY'S FLIGHT REPRESENTED MORE THAN JUST A SUCCESSFUL PROJECT --

IT WAS A BLUEPRINT FOR THE FUTURE OF HANDS-ON LEARNING.

Chris Steinbrook, Superintendent of Schools:

"Well, this is exactly what career technical education should look like, so our students over the, in the course of 2 years, were able to work with the most skilled aviation professionals in the world to build this airplane."

THE CORPUS CHRISTI ARMY DEPOT PROVIDED EXPERTISE AND GUIDANCE TO HELP MAKE THIS DREAM A REALITY, AND FOR DEPOT COMMANDER COLONEL KEVIN CONSTANTINE, THE PARTNERSHIP REFLECTS THE COMMUNITY'S DEEP AVIATION ROOTS.

Colonel Kevin Constantine, Corpus Christi Army Depot:

"Uh, so aviation maintenance and aviation sustainment is in the blood of this community, and that's what we're seeing out here today."
