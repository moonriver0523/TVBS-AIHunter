陸讓伊朗直接控制衛星 美專家:關鍵情資看光

（圖／達志影像美聯社）

美國媒體報導，中國大陸提供衛星影像，讓伊朗掌握美軍動向，發動空襲，專家認為非同小可，因為這次大陸企業甚至讓伊朗直接控制衛星，可隨心所欲掌握關鍵情資。##

SB #CNN 4/16
金融時報記者 Demetri Sevastopulo：「過去我們看過中國衛星公司向伊朗提供影像—也就是傳送照片。這次不同之處在，伊朗與一家相對新的中國公司Emposat（Emposat）簽約，讓伊朗軍方不僅取得存取權，還能有效控制一顆衛星。他們下達任務指令，指定衛星何時何地成像。我們取得他們的任務資訊並以軌道數據交叉比對，發現他們對區域內美軍目標進行俯視—特別是上個月—之後對其中部分目標發動攻擊。」
070948-071038
In recent years…we’ve previously seen Chinese satellite companies providing imagery to Iran—pictures they transmit. What’s different here is that Iran signed a contract with a relatively new Chinese company called Emposat that gives the Iranian military not just access but effective control of a satellite. They input task orders, telling the satellite where and when to look. We obtained information about what they tasked and corroborated it with orbital data. We found they looked down on U.S. targets in the region—particularly last month—and then struck some of those targets afterward on them.

美軍退役海軍艦長 Carl Schuster：「但還取決於飛彈精度。對移動目標，必須預判10到12分鐘後艦艇所在位置，可能相距1,200到1,500碼（約1,097至1,372公尺）。若彈頭圓公誤差仍在10到30公尺，仍可能落空。此外，軍艦—尤其是配備神盾系統的軍艦—可攔截來襲飛彈。因此這是威脅，但不如對機場等固定目標嚴重。伊朗的策略是造成損害；每擊中一個關鍵平台—甚至擦邊—都在美國內部傳遞政治訊息。像RC‑135或E‑3這類機型已停產，一旦被摧毀，替換需要多年。這就是政治訊息—該這樣看待。」
071308-071352
but remember missile accuracy matters. Against a moving target, you have to aim where the ship will be 10 to 12 minutes ahead, which could be 1,200 to 1,500 yards away. If the missile’s warhead is only accurate to within 10 to 30 meters, you still may miss. Plus, a warship—particularly an Aegis-class—can intercept incoming missiles. So it’s a threat, but not as serious as against fixed targets like airfields. Iran’s strategy is to inflict damage; every critical platform they hit—or even a near miss—delivers a political message in the United States.

**07:09:18 (Erin Bernad，主播)**

沒錯，至今未見緩解。迪米特里・薩瓦斯托普洛斯（Demetri Sevastopulo），你是金融時報的美中事務記者。你帶來我在節目開頭提到的重要新訊息：伊朗正使用中國的間諜衛星鎖定美軍基地。請解釋你掌握的內容與其重要性。

Right, which has not abated. Dimitri Savastopoulos, you’re the U.S.–China correspondent for the Financial Times, and you’ve got important new reporting I mentioned at the top of the show: Iran is using a Chinese spy satellite to target U.S. bases. Explain what you know and why it matters.

---

**07:09:49 (Dimitri Savastopoulos，記者)**

重點在於，中國正讓伊朗得以控制一顆專供其用的衛星，這點意義重大。

 China is providing Iran access to control a satellite dedicated to them, and that’s significant.

---

**07:10:47 (Erin Bernad，主播)**

沒錯—這不同於僅提供照片。此舉讓伊朗能控制衛星，拍下用於瞄準的所需畫面。你提到之後遭攻擊的美軍基地。我們來看你們標示的關鍵觀測點地圖。其一是沙烏地阿拉伯的蘇丹王子空軍基地，伊朗至少兩度以驚人精度打擊，不僅擊中跑道上的一架軍機，還擊中該機的操控部位。這些攻擊如何運用衛星？

Right—and that’s distinct from China simply providing pictures. This lets Iran control the satellite to take the pictures they want for targeting. You mentioned U.S. bases that were later targeted. Let’s put up your map of key sites the satellite observed. One crucial place is the Prince Sultan Air Base in Saudi Arabia, which Iran has targeted at least twice, with devastating precision—hitting not just a plane on the runway but the part of the plane where the controls were. How was the satellite used for those strikes?

---

**07:11:42 (Dimitri Savastopoulos，記者)**

依我們取得並以衛星軌道驗證的資料，3月13、14、15日，T‑01B衛星在觀測蘇丹王子空軍基地。我們相信對五架加油機的攻擊發生於3月13日，因此他們對一個主要美軍目標進行了高精度觀測。伊朗雖有自有衛星能力，但解析度相對差。這顆中國衛星的解析度至少高10倍—可達約0.5公尺—使其能精準瞄準特定目標。

Based on what we received and what we verified by tracking the satellite’s orbit, on March 13th, 14th, and 15th the T-01B satellite was looking at Prince Sultan Air Base. We believe the attack on five refueling tankers happened on March 13th, so they had a precise look at a major American target. Iran has some satellite capability, but with relatively poor resolution. This Chinese satellite’s resolution is at least 10 times better—down to about half a meter—giving a strong ability to target specific things.

---

**07:12:29 (Erin Bernad，主播)**

能擊中飛機的特定部位，關鍵就在這裡。舒斯特上校，白宮表示中國並未向伊朗提供武器—或許不把衛星歸類為武器。依迪米特里的描述，伊朗能否用這顆衛星鎖定執行港口封鎖的美艦？以0.5公尺解析度，看來可行。

When you talk about hitting the exact part of a plane, that’s critical. Captain Schuster, the White House says China isn’t supplying weapons to Iran—perhaps choosing not to characterize a satellite as a weapon. Can Iran use this satellite, as Dimitri describes, to target U.S. ships enforcing the port blockade? At half-meter resolution, it seems very doable.

---

**07:13:06 (Carl Schuster，受訪者)**

We no longer produce RC-135s or E-3s, so each one taken out is years from replacement. It’s a political message—think of it that way.
