06:07:46
(弗羅曼) 很高興見到你，吉姆。首先，如果可以的話，在我談到貿易之前，鑑於你在政府中有著悠久的歷史，包括經濟角色，這個政府依賴事實的經濟數據來做政策決策。市場依賴事實的經濟數據來做投資決策。總統攻擊他不喜歡的數字，甚至因此解雇勞工統計局局長，這對系統的信心有什麼影響？
(Froman) Good to see you, Jim. First, if I can, before I get to trade, just given you have a long history in government, including in economic roles, this government relies on factual economic data to make policy decisions. The markets rely on factual economic data to make investment decisions. What is the damage of a president attacking numbers that he just doesn't like, right? And even firing the head of the BLS as a result. What does that do to confidence in the system?

06:08:18
(弗羅曼) 這些數字非常重要。如你所說，市場參與者、企業等非常依賴這些數字。幸運的是，我們有其他數據來源，私營部門有很多分析能力，聯邦儲備系統也有。但勞工統計局一直是那些機構之一，無論是共和黨還是民主黨政府，都努力提供基於事實的中立、無偏見的報告。我們仍然需要這種能力來報告這些事實，無論政府一個月喜歡它們還是下個月不喜歡它們。
(Froman) Well, these numbers are very important. And as you said, the market participants, businesses, others very much rely on these numbers. Now, luckily, we've got other sources of numbers, the private sector has a lot of analytic capacity, the Federal Reserve does as well. But the Bureau of Labor Statistics has been one of those institutions that through Republican and Democratic administrations has worked to provide neutral, unbiased reporting based on the facts. And it's going to be important that we still have that capacity to report those facts, whether the administration likes them one month or dislikes them the next month.

06:09:03
(主播) 你和許多人一樣，一直在衡量川普貿易戰和增加關稅的影響。我已經在這個節目中與很多人交談了數週和數月，包括小型企業、中型企業、大型企業和許多國家的官員。許多國家的官員說，聽著，數字現在看起來可能不錯，但當真正的影響來臨時，將會有經濟損害。我想知道，你是否在這些最新的就業數據中看到關稅與經濟影響之間的滯後期結束？
(主持人) You, like many others, have been measuring the effects of Trump's trade war and increased tariffs. And I've been speaking on this program to a whole host of folks for weeks and months now, small businesses, medium-sized businesses, large businesses, and many countries. The officials from many countries are saying, listen, the numbers may look good now, but when the real effects strike, there will be economic damage. And I wonder, do you see in these latest jobs figures kind of the end of that lag period between tariffs and economic effect?

06:09:40
(弗羅曼) 我認為就業數據的一個解釋是，由於世界各地的不確定性，許多企業在做出是否僱用新員工或投資新業務或新工廠的決定之前都在觀望。我認為現在關稅變得更加明確，方向已經明確，無論特定水平或特定日期如何，我們知道我們將生活在一個關稅更高的世界中。現在企業必須決定這如何影響他們的商業模式，他們是否能夠擴展業務？他們是否需要擴展業務並僱用更多人？關稅實際上生效和在通脹中顯現出來之間往往有滯後期，這些關稅中的許多尚未生效。它們將在7日或可能在12日中國休戰結束時或與墨西哥的未來生效。關稅生效和在通脹中顯現出來以及在增長放緩中顯現出來之間往往有滯後期。但大多數經濟學家預計，如果通過關稅提高價格，將對通脹、生產力和全球增長產生影響，包括其他國家購買我們出口產品的能力。
(Froman) Well, I think one of the explanations of the job numbers is that a lot of businesses sat on the sidelines given all the uncertainty of going around in the world before they had made decisions as to hire new employees or whether to invest in new businesses or new factories. I think now that the tariffs are becoming clearer, certainly the direction of travel is clear without respect to a particular level or a particular date, we know we're gonna be living in a world with higher tariffs. Now businesses are gonna just have to decide how does that affect their business models and can they afford to expand their business? Do they need to expand their business and hire more people? There's tends to be a lag between when tariffs actually get imposed. And of course, a lot of these tariffs haven't been imposed yet. They'll be imposed on the 7th or perhaps on the 12th when the China truce is over or down the road with Mexico. And there tends to be a lag between when they're imposed and when they show up in inflation and as well as when they show up in slower growth. But most economists expect that if you raise prices through tariffs, it's going to have an effect on inflation, on productivity, and on global growth, including the ability of other countries to buy our exports.

06:10:58
(主播) 你撰寫了一篇專欄文章，談到關稅可能會損害川普的關稅目標，包括再工業化，這將削弱進口替代，也削弱工作機會回流。你能解釋為什麼嗎？
(主持人) You wrote an op-ed talking about how the tariffs are likely to damage Trump's goals of these tariffs, including re-industrialization, that it will undermine import substitution and also undermine reshoring jobs. Can you explain why that is?

06:11:20
(弗羅曼) 我們從世界其他地區進口的商品中有一半不是消費品，而是製造業的投入品。因此，美國的公司和工廠依賴於來自世界各地的零件，這些零件被納入他們的製造過程中。當供應鏈被中斷，無法進口或以非常高的價格進口時，會影響他們的競爭能力。他們無法以相同的價格生產相同的產品，因此實際上使再工業化變得更加困難。總統有一個理論，認為在關稅壁壘後，企業最終將被迫將生產轉移到美國，將供應鏈轉移到美國。這可能會發生也可能不會發生，但如果發生，可能需要數年時間。與此同時，這些關稅的成本，人們將在消費品的通脹中非常明顯地看到，而製造商已經在投入成本增加方面看到了這一點。這可能是你提到的製造業就業人數最近減少26,000人的原因之一。因此，我們尚未看到總統希望通過關稅實現的製造業增長，至少目前還沒有。
(Froman) Well, a lot about half of what we import from the rest of the world aren't consumer goods, they're inputs into manufacturing. So companies, factories in the United States relying on parts coming in from around the world that they put into their manufacturing process. When that supply chain is disrupted, either prevented from coming in or coming in at a very high price, it affects their ability to compete. They can't produce the same products at the same price, and so it actually makes it more difficult to re-industrialize. Now, the President has a theory of the case that behind a wall of tariffs, companies will ultimately be forced to move their production to the United States, move their supply chains to the United States. That may or may not happen, but if it does, it may be years from now. In the meantime, the cost of those tariffs, people are going to see them quite visibly in the nature of inflation on consumer goods, and manufacturers are already seeing it in terms of the increased costs of inputs. And that may be one of the reasons why, as you noted, there's been a 26,000 person reduction in manufacturing employment recently. So we're not seeing the increase in manufacturing that the president hoped to achieve with tariffs, at least not yet.

06:12:40
(主播) 你還指出，例如在人工智慧基礎設施方面，現在對人工智慧競賽的關注如此之多，對銅的關稅將影響美國在這方面的領導地位，因為銅是人工智慧基礎設施的重要投入。
(主持人) You also make the point that, for instance, with AI infrastructure, of course, there's so much attention now on the AI race that tariffs, for instance, on copper will impact U.S. leadership there because there's such a big copper input to AI infrastructure.

06:13:00
(弗羅曼) 沒錯。我指出川普的一個目標與另一個目標之間存在緊張關係。這是政策制定的一個挑戰，就是要將這些點連接起來。因此，如果你對銅徵收50%的關稅，這是總統宣布的，至少對某些銅產品來說，銅是數據中心電力基礎設施的絕對重要投入。我們現在正處於數據中心投資的巨大繁榮期，而以合理價格獲得銅的短缺是瓶頸問題之一。因此，如果你徵收關稅，可能需要數年時間來開發美國的新礦山和新的銅精煉能力，如果我們能做到的話。與此同時，我們可能會使我們在與中國競爭人工智慧領導地位的競賽中更加困難。
(Froman) That's right. I mean, I was pointing out that there are tensions between one Trump objective and another Trump objective. And that's one of the challenges of policymaking is to connect the dots here. So if you put 50 percent tariffs on copper, which is what the president has announced, at least on certain copper products, copper is an absolutely integral input into the electricity infrastructure that data centers need. And we're in a huge boom right now of investing in data centers. And the shortage of copper at a reasonable price is one of the choke point issues. So if you put tariffs on, it's gonna take years to develop new mines in the United States, a new refining capacity for copper, if we can do it at all. In the meantime, we may make it more difficult for us to compete in the race with China for leadership in the AI area.
