STORY: Lionel Messi's father, Jorge, died on Friday (August 7) night aged 68 at a medical clinic in the Argentine city of Rosario after a long illness, the footballer's family confirmed to Reuters.
    Jorge spent his final months alternating between a medical center in Rosario and his home, accompanied by his wife Celia and his children Rodrigo, Matias and Maria Sol, the family said.
    Rosario local residents told Reuters on Saturday (August 8) they felt saddened by the news, expressing that Messi has brought them so much joy over the years and that they now wanted to "accompany" the family through this difficult moment.
    Jorge closely accompanied his son Lionel throughout his career, beginning with his early years at Barcelona. He acted as a crucial pillar of support and, for several years, served as his representative.
   Messi's youth team Newell's Old Boys, along with the Argentine FA expressed condolences to the family.
