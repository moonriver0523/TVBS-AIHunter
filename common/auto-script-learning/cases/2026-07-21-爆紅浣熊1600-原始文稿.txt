--REPORTER PKG-AS FOLLOWS--
"what am I looking at?"
THAT IS A GREAT QUESTION - AND THE CONFUSION COULD BE FORGIVEN--
AS VIDEOS OF AN ODDLY-SHAPED RACCOON WANDERING AROUND SEATTLE--
HAVE TAKEN THE INTERNET BY STORM.
"Almost a little supernatural."
THE CONFOUNDING CRITTER, KNOWN TO LOCALS--
FOR ITS UNIQUE AND SPHERICAL SHAPE--
HAS BEEN CAUGHT ON CAMERA ALL AROUND TOWN--
AND WAS NICKNAMED "JIMOTHY" BY AT LEAST ONE WITNESS
WHY, JIMOTHY?
"Me and my husband saw him and he looked like a Jimothy to us"
FAIR ENOUGH. HE LOOKS LIKE SOMETHING- THAT'S FOR SURE.
BUT WHAT GIVES JIMOTHY THIS ODD--
I MEAN, WHAT EXACTLY GIVES HIM...UH, --
......WHAT GIVES?
ONE LOCAL VETERINARIAN BELIEVES JIMOTHY HAS A CONGENITAL SPINE-SHORTENING DISEASE.
"It does look quite unusual, but it is a condition that occurs in other mammals as well."
THATS' NOT TO MINIMIZE JIMOTHY'S EXCEPTIONALITY THOUGH- FOLKS ONLINE SURE AREN'T--
WHERE THE ROTUND AND RASCALLY RACCOON HAS ACHIEVED LEGENDARY STATUS--
COMPLETE WITH ITS OWN FANTASTICAL FAN ART.

ANOTHER FURRY FRIEND GOT A WHOLE LOT OF ATTENTION IN COASTAL CALIFORNIA--
WHEN RESCUERS PULLED OUT ALL THE STOPS TO SAVE A SMALL DOG--
SPOTTED SWIMMING ALONE IN THE SAN FRANCISCO BAY.
WITNESSES ALERTED THE MENLO PARK FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT--
SAYING THE DOG HAD RUN INTO THE WATER- AND JUST KEPT GOING.
AFTER SPOTTING THE POOCH- AN EFFORT WAS LAUNCHED BY AIR AND SEA--
AND THE TEENY TREADING PUP WAS SCOOPED UP AND BROUGHT BACK TO DRY LAND.
THE DOG WAS SHIVERING- BUT ITS HEROES WERE CHIVALROUS--
GIVING IT PLENTY OF LOVE AND WARMTH UNTIL A LOCAL SPCA WAS CONTACTED--
WHICH LATER REUNITED THE DOG WITH ITS FAMILY--
WHO REPORTEDLY HAD BEEN LOOKING FOR IT SINCE JULY 4TH.
