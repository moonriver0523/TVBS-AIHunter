--LEAD IN--
AN AUSTRALIAN YOUNG FAMILY HAS WELCOMED FOUR IDENTICAL BABY GIRLS IN A RARE BIRTH...
DOCTORS BELIEVE IT MAY BE THE FIRST NATURALLY CONCEIVED PREGNANCY OF ITS KIND IN THE COUNTRY.
AFFILIATE SEVEN NETWORK'S MICHAEL HAMMOND HAS THE DETAILS.

--REPORTER PKG-AS FOLLOWS--
TOO TINY FOR THEIR PARENTS' ARMS JUST YET…
THESE 4 LITTLE GIRLS ARE PROOF THE RAREST BEGINNINGS CAN HAVE THE BRIGHTEST FUTURES.

Alexa Bendall, Obstetrician, Royal Brisbane and Women’s Hospital: They have come from one fertilized egg that has split 4 times, which is incredible.

OCCURRING IN JUST 1 IN 15 MILLION PREGNANCIES…
IT'S AN AUSTRALIAN FIRST AND ONE OF ONLY A FEW CASES WORLDWIDE.

Dr. Simone Campbell, Queensland Fertility Group: To share a placenta just means that that splitting has happened relatively late in the embryo life, so exceptionally rare.

PARENTS JANITA AND JORTHAM ALREADY HAD 4 KIDS…
ROLLING THE DICE ONE MORE TIME.

Alexa Bendall: When they found out that they had 4 babies instead of 1, they were quite shocked.

THE PREGNANCY IS EXTREMELY COMPLEX.

Alexa Bendall: And there is a high risk of mortality. And morbidity for these babies and for mum.

BUT THANKS TO THE ROYAL BRISBANE AND WOMEN'S HOSPITAL---
EMILY, HARRIET, ALEXA, AND CATHERINE WERE SAFELY DELIVERED BY C-SECTION AT 28 WEEKS.
EACH WEIGHING JUST OVER 1 KILOGRAMME.

Michael Hammond, Seven Network Reporter: For now, the girls remain in the neonatal intensive care unit, growing stronger every day. In a few months. When they've reached full term, they should be ready to go home.

FROM MUM, ONE LITTLE HAND, ONE GIANT MIRACLE.
“Every day our miracle babies grow stronger is another answered prayer. We love you more than words can ever say.”

Alexa Bendall: Long-term, their outlook will be really good.
