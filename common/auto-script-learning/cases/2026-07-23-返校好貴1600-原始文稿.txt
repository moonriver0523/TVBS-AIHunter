--LEAD IN--
THE START OF THE NEW SCHOOL YEAR IS AROUND THE CORNER. AND FAMILIES ARE ALREADY SHOPPING FOR SCHOOL SUPPLIES. BUT SOME OF THE ITEMS ON YOUR KID’S LIST MAY BE MUCH MORE EXPENSIVE THIS YEAR. IN TODAY’S CONSUMER WATCH, JENN SULLIVAN LOOKS AT HOW MUCH FAMILIES ARE EXPECTED TO SPEND AND WAYS YOU CAN SAVE.

--REPORTER PKG-AS FOLLOWS--
SPENDING ON BACK-TO-SCHOOL SHOPPING IS EXPECTED TO BREAK RECORDS THIS YEAR—TOPPING $43 BILLION ($43.3 Billion) ACCORDING TO A NEW SURVEY FROM THE NATIONAL RETAIL FEDERATION. SHOPPERS PLAN TO SPEND JUST OVER $863 ON SUPPLIES FOR STUDENTS IN KINDERGARTEN THROUGH 12TH GRADE THIS YEAR. THAT’S ABOUT $5 MORE THAN LAST YEAR ($863.86) ACCORDING TO THE SURVEY.

Mark Mathews, National Retail Federation, Chief Economist and Executive Director of Research: “I think there’s definitely an expectation that prices are going to be higher for certain goods.”

MARK MATHEWS WITH THE N-R-F SAYS THE COST OF ELECTRONICS AND SHOES IS DRIVING UP PRICES THIS YEAR. HERE’S THE BREAKDOWN OF WHERE THAT MONEY IS GOING.

ELECTRONICS – ABOUT 293 DOLLARS
CLOTHING - 250 DOLLARS
SHOES - 174 DOLLARS
AND SCHOOL SUPPLIES ABOUT 146 DOLLARS.

MATHEWS SAYS THE GOOD NEWS IS RETAILERS ARE ALREADY ROLLING OUT SALES AND DISCOUNTS ON SCHOOL SUPPLIES. HE SAYS THAT’S ONE WAY TO HELP SAVE MONEY.

Mark Mathews, National Retail Federation, Chief Economist and Executive Director of Research: “One thing that helps you with is to spread that spending across the entire season because these are not small sums of money that people are having to spend to get their kids back to school.”

COLLEGE STUDENTS CAN EXPECT TO SPEND EVEN MORE. SHOPPERS PLAN TO SPEND AN AVERAGE OF $1400 ($1,437.79) ON BACK-TO-SCHOOL ITEMS FOR COLLEGE. THAT’S $111 MORE THAN LAST YEAR.

MATHEWS SAYS THE MAIN REASON IS COMPUTER PRICES.

Mark Mathews, National Retail Federation, Chief Economist and Executive Director of Research: “If we look at computers, we know that, chip prices are incredibly high and that’s pushing some of the cost of computers and this is a big ticket item.”

MANY TOP MANUFACTURERS OFFER PAYMENT PLANS FOR COMPUTERS. AND SOME EVEN OFFER STUDENT DISCOUNTS BETWEEN 5-10%. THAT CAN HELP YOU BUDGET AND SAVE.

FOR CONSUMER WATCH, I’M JENN SULLIVAN.
